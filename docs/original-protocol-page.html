Phase 1: 100% Biga Preferment

Ragged cluster hydration & dual-stage temperature hold

Mon 8:00 PM 0/4 Done
1. Dissolve IDY & Load Bowl
Mon 8:00 PM 3 mins

Dissolve 1.6 g IDY in 574 g cool water (58–60 °F). Pour over 1275 g Caputo Cuoco in spiral mixer. The extra yeast buffers freeze mortality.

2. Coarse Cluster Mix (Speed 1, 90–120s Max)
Mon 8:05 PM CRITICAL

Mix on Speed 1 for 90 to 120s maximum. Stop as soon as loose flour is absorbed. Biga must look like damp walnut-sized rubble—never ball up.

3. Ambient Yeast Awakening Rest
Mon 8:10 PM – 12:00 AM 3.5–4 hrs

Transfer loosely to an oiled tub. Do not pack down. Rest at room temp (68–70 °F) for 3.5 to 4 hours until clusters swell ~30% and emit sweet, yeasty aromas.

4. Cold Ferment in 37 °F Fridge
Tue 12:00 AM – 5:00 PM ~17 hrs

Place tub into 37 °F fridge. Cold preserves Cuoco's strong gluten matrix while allowing deep enzymatic sugar generation without excess acetic acid.

Phase 2: Final Mix & Staged Bassinage

Ice water dosing, salt wash-in, and strict FDT hard stop

Tue 5:00 PM 0/4 Done
1. Tear Biga & Run Dry
Tue 5:00 PM 60s

Tear chilled biga into 8–10 cold chunks directly into spiral mixer bowl. Run dry on Speed 1 for 60s with NO water to break up the chunks.

2. Staged Bassinage (200 g Ice Water)
Tue 5:03 PM 3 doses

Switch to Speed 2. Drizzle ~200 g ice-cold water down the bowl wall in 3 slow additions. Allow the dough to absorb each dose and re-grip the spiral before adding the next.

3. Salt (36 g) & Final Wash-In (119 g Ice Water)
Tue 5:08 PM 2 mins

When dough clears the bowl, sprinkle 36 g fine sea salt. Immediately drizzle remaining 119 g ice water over the salt to wash it into the interface.

4. High-Speed Windowpane & FDT Hard Stop
Tue 5:12 PM STOP: 77 °F

Mix on Speed 4–5 for 3–5 minutes until smooth and windowpaning. Target FDT: 73–75 °F. If the dough reaches 77 °F (25 °C), STOP mixing immediately to protect enzymes.

Phase 3: Bench Handling & The Capuano Oil Veil

Coil folds, topical olive oil barrier (*un velo d'olio*), and dry-seam balling

Tue 5:30 PM 0/4 Done
1. Rest 20m & Coil Fold #1
Tue 5:30 PM 20 mins

Rest bulk dough 20 mins. With wet hands, lift from center, let ends curl underneath, rotate 90°, and repeat once.

2. Rest 20m & Coil Fold #2
Tue 5:50 PM 20 mins

Perform second coil fold. Dough should stand tall with visible surface tension.

3. Apply Capuano Oil Veil (*Un Velo d'Olio*)
Tue 6:15 PM CAPUANO

Turn bulk dough onto counter into a smooth dome. Drizzle 10–12 g EVOO across the top and smooth into a micro-thin, glossy lipid film. Invert the tub over the dough and rest undisturbed for 30 minutes to let Cuoco relax. (Do not laminate).

4. Ball on Dry Counter (8 × 275 g)
Tue 6:45 PM 8 × 275 g

Cut into 8 × 275 g portions. Keep bottom counter DRY. Drag-and-cup each ball so friction pulls the oiled skin taut while the un-oiled bottom seam seals completely airtight.

3. Gas Migration Finger Press & Knuckle Stretch
Fri 5:00 PM 1 min

Hydration Allocation
