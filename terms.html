What this is

Canotto Lab is a free, open source calculator and bake log. There is no account, no subscription and nothing to buy. It is offered as it is, and it may change or stop being available at any time.

Your data is yours

Your recipes and bakes are stored in your browser, and in your own Google account if you choose to sync. We claim no ownership of anything you write and we have no copy of it. What we do and do not collect is set out in the privacy statement.

Because your library lives in your browser, clearing your browser data will delete it. Downloading the JSON backup is the way to keep it, and doing so is your responsibility rather than ours.
